- Attention-benchmark timing (v2 H100 grid): K ∈ {0, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128}
  all measured; the appendix detail figure (b) renders K ∈ {2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64}
  (K=128 rows exist in the CSV but are not rendered).
- Training-step benchmark (`speed_mem.csv`): K ∈ {0, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64}.
- Tracking accuracy (K-sweep): dense + K ∈ {2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64} trained and
  evaluated under the corrected 5-condition protocol (trpL held out);
  **K=128 has no training run** (at vanvliet token counts K=128 ≥ N for most
  windows, i.e. KNN degenerates to dense — benchmark-only by design).
