This week I took the time to recheck my implementation. 
The previously implementation was still working with knn_masking and therefore was not able to leverage flash attention.
The new implementation gets rid of the masks entirely.

I reran all my tests. Some of the results were the same, some are different. 

Most important insights:
1. Running on F32 does not dispatch CUDNN kernel F16 does (Flash Attention Sanity Check)
2. The Nk outperforms N² at arround 2028 tokens (same findings as in previous implementation)
3. Max Speedup for 2^13 with 5.88x and 18.3x less memory. Some of the configs could not be run due to OOM.
4. The token-reordering could only achieve a speedup of 1.03x with my current implementation (Hassani)

The full report of the findings together with the reimplementation of the gather based attention can be found in the pdf.
