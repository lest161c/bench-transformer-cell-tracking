5. SSL: Downstream Transfer

Encoder-Freeze Test: NT-Xent vs BCE Mismatch

Frozen encoder: SSL (0.625) slightly below random (0.709). Full training: SSL (0.745) substantially above random (0.500). Encoder adapts +0.120 during downstream — NT-Xent features not directly usable for cross-attention BCE.

SSL Mode Comparison

NoPE (0.625) = Shuffle (0.625) > FourierPE (0.609) > Temporal (0.500) = Random (0.500). Removing positional encoding during SSL provides the clearest benefit. Coordinate shuffling yields same result at higher complexity. Temporal SSL shows no transfer.

Strategy Scorecard: Transfer Efficiency (Δ over random baseline)

Δ over random init (0.500). NoPE +0.245 > Shuffle +0.125.

Scale Ceiling: SSL benefit shrinks with model capacity

Small scale (2L/2L): NoPE SSL +0.245 above random. Large scale (3L/3L, 60 frames): random init catches up, SSL provides no advantage.
